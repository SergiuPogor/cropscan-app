Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Short version: CropScan works entirely on your device. It has no accounts and makes no network connections. Your scans, receipts, invoices and recognised text never leave your phone unless you choose to share or export them.

1. Who we are

CropScan (“the app”) is developed and published by MEDIAPRONET SRL (IDNO 1012600005500, str. N. Dimo 1/2, of. 20, MD-2068, Chișinău, Republic of Moldova; CEO: Serghei Pogor) (“we”, “us”). You can reach us at mediapronetsrl@gmail.com.

2. Data we collect

None. CropScan does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. There are no user accounts, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and the app does not connect to the internet to process your documents.

3. Where your content is stored

Everything you scan or import (including PDF files you open with CropScan) — document images, receipts, invoices, extracted text, folders and settings — is stored only in CropScan’s private storage on your device. This storage is not accessible to other apps, and CropScan does not upload it anywhere. The app’s data is excluded from Android’s automatic cloud backup.

4. Camera

The camera is used solely to capture the documents you choose to scan. Captured images are processed on your device (edge detection, cropping, enhancement and text recognition) and are never sent to us or any third party.

5. Text recognition (OCR)

Text recognition runs fully on your device using a bundled, offline model. The text extracted from your scans stays on your device.

6. Permissions we request

  • Camera — to photograph documents. Required for scanning.
  • Biometric / device credential — only if you enable App Lock. Authentication is handled by Android’s secure system; CropScan never sees your fingerprint, face or PIN.
  • Add to Contacts — the business-card feature opens Android’s own “add contact” screen pre-filled with the scanned details. CropScan does not read your contacts.
  • Notifications — only if you set a warranty on a receipt or an expiry date on a document (ID, passport, licence, policy), to remind you before it ends. Reminders are scheduled on your device; nothing is sent anywhere.

7. Sharing and exporting

When you share or export a file (PDF, image, text, Excel, ZIP, or a backup), you pick the destination app or service through Android’s share sheet or file picker. From that point the file is handled by the app or service you selected, under their privacy policy. CropScan does not control what that third party does with it.

8. Purchases and donations

CropScan Pro is not available to buy yet. When it is, purchases will be handled entirely by Google Play under Google’s privacy policy; CropScan only receives confirmation of whether you have Pro, never your payment details.

Versions of CropScan distributed outside Google Play may show optional donation links. If you use one, the app opens an external page (Ko-fi or PayPal) in your browser. Any payment is handled entirely by that provider under their own terms and privacy policy; CropScan does not receive or process your payment information, and donating does not unlock any feature.

9. Children

CropScan is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above and post the new version at this URL.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email mediapronetsrl@gmail.com.
