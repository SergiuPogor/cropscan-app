One scan takes about three seconds

No cropping by hand and no fiddly settings. CropScan does the boring part and leaves you in control of the result.

  1. Point

    A green outline locks onto the page in real time, even on a busy desk or with a finger over a corner.

  2. Hold still

    Focus and exposure lock on the page. When the outline is steady and the text is sharp, it takes the shot on its own.

  3. Check the corners

    Drag any corner if you want to. A magnifier shows exactly where the edge is. Then the page is straightened to its real shape.

  4. Share

    Save as PDF, JPEG or ZIP and send it to Viber, Telegram, WhatsApp, Gmail or Drive in one tap.

Le Chatelier's principle: when a system at equilibrium is disturbed, it shifts to undo the change.

Raising the pressure favours the side with fewer gas molecules. Adding heat favours the endothermic direction.

Questions people ask

Is CropScan really free?

Yes. Scanning, filters, PDF and image export, text recognition, folders, app lock and backups are all free, with no ads and no watermark.

The receipt tracker includes 10 receipt scans a month for free. A paid option for heavy receipt users may come later. Everything else stays free.

Does it need the internet?

No. Everything, including text recognition, runs on your phone. It works in airplane mode, in a basement archive or abroad without roaming. The only time a browser opens is if you tap a donation link.

Which phones does it work on?

Any Android phone with a camera running Android 7.0 or later.

What if it can't find the edges of my page?

It never gets in your way. Take the shot with the shutter button, and CropScan lets you place the four corners yourself with a magnifier. For the best automatic results, put light paper on a darker surface.

Can I make one PDF from many pages?

Yes. Scan pages one after another, reorder them, then export a single PDF. You can also select several saved documents and merge them into one.

Which languages can it read?

Text recognition reads languages written in the Latin alphabet, such as English, Romanian, French, German, Spanish and Italian. Scanning itself works with any language, since it's just the image.

Where are my scans kept, and how do I move them to a new phone?

They're kept in CropScan's private storage on your phone. To move them, go to Settings, then Backup and restore, export a backup file, copy it to the new phone and restore it there.

How do I delete my data?

Delete documents inside the app, or uninstall CropScan to remove everything at once. There's no copy on any server, because there's no server.

I found a bug or have an idea. Who do I tell?

Email mediapronetsrl@gmail.com. Tell us your phone model and what happened. Every message is read.
